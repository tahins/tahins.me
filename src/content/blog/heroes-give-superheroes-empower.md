We often consider giving someone something is the highest form of help. However, it’s not right. 

Don’t get me wrong. I am not against charity or giving something to someone needy. As a Muslim charity is a big part of our obligation to our creator. It’s right of the have-nots over the haves. Giving charity does make someone hero, no doubt, but not a superhero. 

A superhero, on the other hand empowers people with skills or whatever someone needs. It has more impact and is more sustainable. 

Let me give you an example. Let’s say a needy person came to you for money. You can either give him the money he needed and become a hero or you can assess his situation and find out how you can empower him.

people want money for a reason. we can fix that reason. for example a man who needs money for his daughter’s marriage. we can ask why does he needs the money for and purchase that for him. they others can contribute on that purchase and even consider that as an gift for that marriage. thus collectiely we can make useful contribution.

also, we can build a collective fund, which anyone in that group can use in emergency situation. thus we can serve peoples need which is higher than his contribution to that fund.
